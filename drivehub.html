DriveHub

A car rental app for finding a vehicle, choosing to self-drive or hire a driver, and booking it, from search to verification, payment and confirmation.

01 · Overview

Renting a car, start to finish.

DriveHub covers the whole rental journey in one place: search by pickup location and dates, compare available vehicles, choose self-drive or a hired driver, then book, verify and pay.

Each screen was designed and handed off with its exact copy, layout anatomy and interaction states, so the product could be built without guessing.

02 · The experience

Browse freely, commit with confidence.

The journey is built to let people look before they commit. Search and browsing stay open to everyone, and the details needed to rent are only asked for once someone is ready to book.

Find a vehicle

One short form for where and when, plus a choice between self-drive and hiring a driver. Results show the daily rate and estimated total up front.

Clear booking details

Green text means you can change it; a plain value means it is calculated. No input boxes or dropdowns where nothing needs typing.

Verify, then pay

Requirements is an overview, not a form: each item says what is needed and where it stands, before a payment screen with the total in view.

03 · Core flow

From search to booked.

The main path takes five screens: find a vehicle, compare the results, check the vehicle, review the booking and get a confirmation with the booking ID.

Vehicle images use a neutral no-photo placeholder on purpose: only real DriveHub vehicle photos are used, never stock or generated images.

04 · Verify and pay

One task per screen.

Verification is split into small steps. Each screen asks for one thing, explains why it is needed and how the document is used, then leads to payment and a read-only view of the booking.

05 · Account

Sign in only when it is needed.

There is no sign-in wall. People are asked to create an account or log in only when an action needs it, and their booking details are kept while they do.

06 · Design process

Designed to be built.

01

Map the journey

Lay out the rental from search to confirmation, including where an account and verification are really needed.

02

Design the screens

Build each screen in Figma from shared components, with one primary action and plain, specific copy.

03

Cover every state

Design the empty, focused, filled, error, loading and offline states for each interaction.

04

Hand off

Document every screen with its exact copy, anatomy and states so it can be built without guesswork.

07 · Key decisions

Small details matter.

Most of DriveHub's decisions are about not asking for too much, too early.

01
No sign-in wall.

Browsing and search stay open. Log in appears only when someone chooses it or an action needs an account.

02
Editable values look different.

Green text on the right means you can change it; a plain value is calculated. No fake input boxes.

03
Real photos only.

Vehicle images are real DriveHub photography or a neutral placeholder, never stock or AI-generated cars.

04
Ask for only what is needed.

Personal details are three fields. Each verification step asks for one document and says how it will be used.
